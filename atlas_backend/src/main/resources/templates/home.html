A visual, interconnected library of human movement designed to make movement knowledge easier to explore, understand, and apply.

What you can do

Movement Library

A structured catalog of movements, each with clear descriptions, practical cues, and visual illustrations, organized with searchable tags.

Visual Representation

Every movement is illustrated step by step in one consistent visual style, so positions and techniques are easy to understand and compare.

Connected Movements

Movements are linked into a graph of progressions, variants and other relationships, so you can see what leads to a movement and where it can take you next.

Routine Building

Organize movements into personalized routines with ordered sections.
