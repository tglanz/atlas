# Practitioner

## Browse Movements

Browse and search the Movement library by name and tags.

## View a Movement

View its description, cues, illustrations, tags, and links to related Movements. Each relationship type appears in its own subsection, such as Progressions, with previews of candidate Movements.

## Choose an Avatar

Choose the kind of figure to see in Movement illustrations. Without a choice, the default Avatar is used.

## Create a Routine

Create a Routine with a name and description, organized into ordered Sections containing specific Movement revisions.

## Edit a Routine

Update the Routine details, manage its Sections, and reorder or replace Movements.

## View a Routine

View the Routine and its ordered Sections and Movements.

# Maintainer

## Create a Movement

Create a Movement draft with its name, description, cues, tags, and links to other Movements. Illustrations are optional during creation.

## Revise a Movement

Create a new draft revision while preserving published revisions.

## Generate Movement Illustrations

For a Movement draft, select an Avatar and a Viewpoint and specify the stages of the movement, each in words or with a reference picture. The system generates one Illustration per stage and replaces that Avatar's sequence on the draft. A failed generation can be retried.

## Upload a Movement Illustration

For a Movement draft, select an Avatar and provide a picture. The picture is stored as is and added to the end of that Avatar's sequence.

## Manage Movement Links

Define directed relationships between Movement Keys. Conceptually bidirectional relationships are represented by links in both directions.
