# Practitioner

## Browse Movements

Find Movements in the Movement Index by name, with suggestions while typing, and by toggling tags.

## View a Movement

View its description, items grouped by type (such as cues and comments), illustrations, tags, and the Movements related to it. Each relationship type appears in its own subsection, such as Progressions, with previews of candidate Movements.

## Create a Routine

Create a Routine with a name and description, organized into ordered Sections containing Movements.

## Edit a Routine

Update the Routine details, manage its Sections, and reorder or replace Movements.

## View a Routine

View the Routine and its ordered Sections and Movements.

# Maintainer

## Create a Movement

Create a Movement with its name, description, items, and tags. Relationships and Illustrations can be added at the same time or afterwards.

## Edit a Movement

Change a Movement's details, relationships, or Illustrations. The change is visible immediately.

## Relate Movements

In the Movement editor, define the Movement's relationships to other existing Movements, each with a type chosen from the offered types, such as progression or variant. Adding a relationship also adds its inverse on the other Movement, for example a regression for a progression. A Movement shows the Movements it is related to in one section per type, such as Progressions.

## Set Movement Illustrations

For a Movement, choose one of two patterns and define the stages of the movement. One Illustration is produced per stage, and the result replaces the Movement's sequence.

- Generate with an Avatar: choose an Avatar and a Viewpoint (the default Viewpoint is preselected), then give each stage instructions, a reference image, or both.
- Use my own images: give each stage an image, which is used as is.
