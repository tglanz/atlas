# Introduction

Form Atlas is a visual, interconnected library of human movement designed to make movement knowledge easier to explore, understand, and apply. It brings movements, instructional cues, illustrations, and relationships together in one structured system, helping practitioners discover new skills, deepen their understanding, and build purposeful routines.

## Movement Library

A structured catalog of movements, each presented with clear descriptions, practical cues, and visual illustrations. Movements are organized with searchable tags and connected through meaningful relationships, making it easier to discover related skills and explore different areas of movement practice.

## Visual Representation

Movements are illustrated through configurable visual representations, with consistent figures and viewpoints that help practitioners understand positions and techniques. The illustration system provides a unified visual language across the library.

## Routine Building

Practitioners can organize movements into personalized routines, grouping them into ordered sections to suit their goals and practice.
